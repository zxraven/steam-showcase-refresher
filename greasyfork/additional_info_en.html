Steam Showcase Refresher

Put an animated artwork showcase on your Steam profile in a few clicks — no hex editors, no console commands, no manual uploads.

What it does

  • Uploads your animated GIFs to Steam and places them into your profile showcase.
  • Works with the Artwork Showcase (two parts), My Workshop Showcase (five strips, any row) and the Workshop Showcase (one picture).
  • Puts sets you uploaded before back into a showcase — no second upload.
  • Helps you review, hide and delete old uploads — only what you choose.
  • Interface in English, Ukrainian and Russian.

How to use

  1. Install the script.
  2. Open your Steam profile on steamcommunity.com — a small Showcase Refresher panel appears in the bottom-right corner.
  3. Choose your GIFs and press Install to my showcase. The script does the rest.

In Chrome, Edge, Opera and other Chromium-based browsers, Tampermonkey needs Allow User Scripts (or Developer mode) turned on in the extension settings.

Privacy and safety

  • Runs only on steamcommunity.com, in your own session.
  • Never asks for your password and sends nothing to third parties.
  • Deletes or hides nothing unless you explicitly confirm it.
