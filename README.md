# Steam Showcase Refresher

**EN** · [UA](#ua) · [RU](#ru)

A userscript (Tampermonkey) that puts an animated showcase on your Steam profile in a few clicks: the **Artwork Showcase** (2 GIFs) or the **Workshop Showcase** (5 strips). It uploads your GIFs through Steam's own forms, places them into the showcase and helps you review and tidy up old uploads — only what you choose.

- **Install:** [Greasy Fork](https://greasyfork.org/scripts/599798-steam-showcase-refresher) — updates arrive automatically.
- **Full guide:** [docs/README.html](docs/README.html) (EN | UA | RU, download and open in a browser).
- **Choose artwork for your profile:** [alexnocturne.blacksea.in.ua](https://alexnocturne.blacksea.in.ua)

Runs only on steamcommunity.com in your own session, never asks for your password and sends nothing to third parties.
